A poem of life
----------------
A man dies and decays in his grave
leaving behind all his achievements and wealth
None will remember him for all that, but only with them how he behave
Everyone will remember him based on with him, how they felt
.----------------
In a graveyard, none will think
whether the deceased lived in a brick kiln
or in mansions made of goldbut among the masses, the story of the deceased's character will be told.
----------------Purpose gives man's life meaning
One who even though doesn't have the most of wealth but has fulfilled his true purpose lawfully
is a true human being.
If he made his life belong to noble character
Everyone will cherish his name eternally
But if man has high wealth but lives his life as a clown
after his death, upon him everyone will frown.
